# PRIVACY NOTICE

 

Last updated: 23 October 2026

 

## INTRODUCTION

 

AFTERTONE is a real-time messaging service designed to facilitate direct communication between two people through invitation-based connections.

 

This Privacy Notice explains how information may be processed when you access or use AFTERTONE, including information associated with connections, technical operations, and message delivery. It also describes the privacy protections provided by the service, their limitations, and your responsibilities as a user.

 

AFTERTONE is designed with privacy in mind. However, no online service can guarantee complete anonymity or eliminate every security risk. Please read this Notice carefully before using the service.

 

## INFORMATION WE PROCESS

 

Depending on how you use AFTERTONE, the service and its hosting infrastructure may process the following categories of information.

 

### CONNECTION INFORMATION

 

AFTERTONE processes information necessary to create and manage invitation-based connections. This may include invitation codes, connection states, temporary session identifiers, and information needed to establish or maintain a connection.

 

### TECHNICAL INFORMATION

 

When you access AFTERTONE, the server and hosting infrastructure may process technical information associated with your request. Depending on the infrastructure and its configuration, this may include your IP address, requested URL, browser information, timestamps, connection events, and error information.

 

Hosting providers and other infrastructure services may independently generate operational or security logs.

 

### MESSAGE INFORMATION

 

AFTERTONE is designed to encrypt message content in the browser before it is relayed through the server.

 

The server may therefore handle encrypted message data without needing access to the original plaintext message. However, information required to route messages and manage connections may still be processed.

 

Encryption does not necessarily conceal all information about communication, such as the fact that a connection exists or when network activity occurs.

 

### INFORMATION YOU CHOOSE TO SHARE

 

Information you voluntarily communicate to another person is shared with that recipient. AFTERTONE does not control what recipients subsequently do with information they receive.

 

## ENCRYPTION AND ITS LIMITATIONS

 

AFTERTONE uses browser-based cryptographic mechanisms intended to protect message content during communication.

 

The implementation uses public-key cryptography to establish shared secret material and symmetric encryption to protect messages. The server acts as a relay for encrypted message data.

 

Encryption provides an important layer of protection, but it has limitations.

 

 
- Encryption does not guarantee that your identity or network activity is anonymous.

 
- Your device, browser, or recipient's device may expose message content.

 
- A recipient may copy, photograph, record, or share messages outside AFTERTONE.

 
- Security may be affected by compromised devices, malicious browser extensions, software vulnerabilities, or other attacks.

 
- If cryptographic keys are exchanged without independently verifying the other party's identity, an active attacker may be able to interfere with the exchange.

 

 

AFTERTONE should not be treated as a guarantee of protection against every possible attacker or surveillance method.

 

## MESSAGE STORAGE AND RETENTION

 

AFTERTONE is designed for real-time communication rather than permanent message-history storage.

 

The service's application-level storage behavior, however, must be distinguished from server logs, hosting infrastructure records, browser storage, and information retained by recipients.

 

Message data may exist temporarily in application memory while being routed or processed. Technical or security logs may be retained separately according to the relevant server and hosting configuration.

 

Information may also remain on a user's device or a recipient's device outside the server's control.

 

We do not claim that all information is immediately or permanently erased when a connection closes unless the relevant systems have been verified to support that behavior.

 

The actual retention of technical records may depend on operational requirements, hosting-provider policies, and applicable legal obligations.

 

## HOW INFORMATION IS USED

 

Information processed in connection with AFTERTONE may be used, where necessary, to:

 

 
- Establish and manage messaging connections.

 
- Validate invitations and connection requests.

 
- Relay encrypted messages to their intended destinations.

 
- Detect replayed or invalid protocol messages.

 
- Maintain service availability and reliability.

 
- Diagnose technical failures and investigate security incidents.

 
- Prevent abuse and protect the service and its users.

 
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

 

 

Information should be used only for purposes consistent with the operation, security, and lawful administration of the service.

 

## DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

 

AFTERTONE does not need to disclose plaintext message content to its server in order to relay encrypted messages. Nevertheless, some information may be accessible to or processed by infrastructure providers or other parties.

 

Information may be disclosed in the following circumstances, where applicable.

 

### HOSTING AND INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDERS

 

The providers responsible for hosting, networking, security, or other operational services may process technical information as necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies and retention practices may also apply.

 

### SECURITY AND ABUSE PREVENTION

 

Information reasonably available to the service may be used to investigate suspected misuse, protect the service, and respond to security incidents.

 

### LEGAL OBLIGATIONS

 

Information may be preserved or disclosed when required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or a lawful request from a competent authority, subject to applicable requirements.

 

The information available for disclosure depends on what the service and its providers actually possess or retain.

 

### SERVICE CHANGES

 

If the ownership or operation of AFTERTONE changes, information may be handled as part of that transition where lawful and appropriate. Any necessary notice or consent will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

 

## SECURITY MEASURES AND LIMITATIONS

 

AFTERTONE uses technical measures intended to protect communications and support reliable service operation.

 

However, internet-based services cannot guarantee absolute security. No method of transmission, storage, or encryption is entirely free from risk.

 

Users should keep their devices and browsers updated, protect access to their devices, avoid sharing invitation links with unintended recipients, and verify the identity of the person with whom they are communicating.

 

AFTERTONE should not be relied upon as the sole protection for highly sensitive information or situations requiring independently verified identities and strong assurances against targeted attacks.

 

## LAWFUL AND RESPONSIBLE USE

 

AFTERTONE is intended for lawful, responsible communication.

 

The service is not intended to facilitate or promote violence, criminal activity, threats, exploitation, or other unlawful conduct.

 

Encryption is a privacy and security measure, not permission to misuse the service. Users remain responsible for ensuring that their activities comply with applicable laws and the service's Terms of Service.

 

Where suspected abuse or unlawful activity requires attention, the service may take reasonable action consistent with its technical capabilities, applicable policies, and legal obligations.

 

## YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

 

When using AFTERTONE, you are responsible for:

 

 
- Using the service lawfully and responsibly.

 
- Ensuring that you intend to communicate with the person associated with an invitation.

 
- Protecting your device, browser, and any information accessible through them.

 
- Understanding that recipients may retain or share messages they receive.

 
- Avoiding the transmission of information that you are not comfortable sharing with the intended recipient.

 
- Reviewing this Privacy Notice and the Terms of Service periodically.

 

 

You should not assume that a message can be recalled or erased from a recipient's device after it has been delivered.

Users can manage cookies and browser storage through their browser settings, although disabling necessary storage may affect how the service operates.
